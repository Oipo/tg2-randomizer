  What I found by following those callers statically (grepping the ROM for writers/readers of $0108):

  1. $81:8147 JSL $80CD09 runs right after the module handoff; at $80:CD2C it computes $0108 = $7E:FE0A*4 + $7E:FE0E (country×4 + race — the same pair the shop keeps in $1CE1/$1CE5). There's a demo-mode override table at $80:F2DD.
  2. $81:8717 (the JSR $8717 at $81:8158) then loads all race graphics using a per-(country,race) descriptor record in bank $87: index table at $87:A656, 64 record-pointer words contiguous at $87:A676-A6F5 (file 0x3A676), records 0x58 bytes each. Record fields are (addr,bank) pairs fed to the $81:DE85
  decompressor — field +$46 → $7E:6200, +$22 → $7E:6400, and +$4A → $7F:0000+, which is what fills the tile buffers that 8885AA streams to VRAM. For country 0/race 0 that source is $8D:C572.
